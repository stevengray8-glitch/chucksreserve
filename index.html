About the Store

Chuck’s Reserve is one of the few remaining independent thrift stores in Garden Grove, located at the heart of Historic Main Street. We keep the spirit of thrifting simple: low prices, clean space, and a constantly rotating selection of clothing, housewares, and everyday finds.

As a co-owned venture under the broader SCG ENTERPRIZES family, the store focuses on community accessibility and straightforward secondhand retail. Inventory turns over regularly from local donations.

Current focus: Clean, priced inventory and consistent hours so regulars know what to expect when they walk in.

Donations

We accept clean, usable clothing, housewares, and small household items during open hours. Please bring items in bags or boxes when possible.

  • Clothing in good condition
  • Small housewares and kitchen items
  • Books, décor, and everyday goods

Large furniture or oversized items: call or text ahead so we can confirm we have space. We may arrange limited pickups depending on location and item size.
